# Booking Changes and Group Bookings

Aurora Bay Hotel (fictional demo property). Version 2026-11.

## Changing dates, room type or guests

You can change the dates, the room type or the number of guests on a booking up to 48 hours before the check-in time, subject to availability. The stay is re-priced at the rates that apply on the day of the change.

## Price differences after a change

If the new price is higher, the difference is charged to the original payment method. If the new price is lower, the difference is refunded automatically within 5 to 10 business days.

## Changes inside 48 hours

Within 48 hours of arrival, changes cannot be made online or by the assistant. Please contact the front desk; a late change may be handled as a cancellation under the cancellation policy plus a new booking.

## Name changes

The lead guest name can be changed free of charge at any time before arrival.

## Group and large bookings

Bookings of 4 or more rooms, or with a total of 3,000 USD or more, are reviewed by a manager before they are confirmed. You will hear back within one business day. A maximum of 8 rooms can be booked in a single reservation; contact the sales team for larger groups.

## Maximum stay

A single reservation can cover up to 28 nights.
