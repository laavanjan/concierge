# Pets and Children

Aurora Bay Hotel (fictional demo property). Version 2026-11.

## Pets

Dogs up to 15 kg are welcome in Standard Queen and Deluxe King Sea View rooms, one dog per room, for 30 USD per night. Pets are not allowed in the Family Suite or the Penthouse Suite. Pets may not be left alone in the room.

## Service animals

Trained service animals are welcome in every room type free of charge.

## Children

Children under 12 stay free when they use the existing bedding. Cots are free on request. Children are counted as guests for room capacity.

## Extra beds

An extra bed is available in the Deluxe King Sea View room for a third guest at 40 USD per night. The Family Suite sleeps four with a separate kids' room and needs no extra bed.
