# Rooms and Suites

Aurora Bay Hotel (fictional demo property). Version 2026-11.

## Standard Queen Room

The Standard Queen Room (22 square metres) has one queen bed, a garden view, a rain shower and a work desk. It sleeps up to 2 guests. Dogs up to 15 kg are allowed. Base rate 140 USD per night.

## Deluxe King Sea View

The Deluxe King Sea View (30 square metres) has one king bed, a balcony and a sea view. It sleeps 2, or 3 with an extra bed for 40 USD per night. Dogs up to 15 kg are allowed. Base rate 210 USD per night.

## Family Suite

The Family Suite (48 square metres) is a two-room suite with one king bed plus two single beds in a separate kids' room, a kitchenette and a garden terrace. It sleeps up to 4 guests. Pets are not allowed. Base rate 320 USD per night.

## Penthouse Suite

The Penthouse Suite (95 square metres) is on the top floor with two king beds, a private terrace, a hot tub and a panoramic sea view. It sleeps up to 4 guests. Pets are not allowed. There is only one penthouse. Base rate 750 USD per night.
