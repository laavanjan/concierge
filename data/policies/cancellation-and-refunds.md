# Cancellation and Refund Policy

Aurora Bay Hotel (fictional demo property). Version 2026-11. Applies to all direct bookings.

## Payment at booking

All direct bookings are prepaid in full when the reservation is confirmed. We send a secure payment link after booking; our team will never ask for card numbers over chat, WhatsApp, email or phone.

## Free cancellation window

You can cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before the check-in time (15:00 hotel time on the arrival date). Cancelling inside the free window refunds 100% of the amount paid.

## Festive peak season

For stays that include any night in the festive peak season (20 December to 5 January), the free cancellation window is longer: you can cancel free of charge until 7 days before the check-in time on the arrival date.

## Late cancellation

If you cancel after the free cancellation window has closed but before the check-in time on the arrival date, the first night of the stay (including tax) is charged and the rest of the amount paid is refunded.

## No-shows

A cancellation made after the check-in time on the arrival date, or a guest who does not arrive, is treated as a no-show. No-shows are not refunded.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Refunds of 1,000 USD or more are reviewed by a duty manager before they are released; this review normally takes less than one business day.

## Goodwill refunds

Refunds outside this policy (for example after a service problem during your stay) are at the discretion of the duty manager. Our assistant can submit the request, and a manager approves or declines it.
