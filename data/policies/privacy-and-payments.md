# Privacy and Payment Security

Aurora Bay Hotel (fictional demo property). Version 2026-11.

## Card details

Never share full card numbers, security codes (CVV) or bank details in chat, WhatsApp, email or on a call with the assistant. Payments are taken only through a secure payment link sent after booking. If you shared card details by mistake, we mask them automatically and recommend contacting your bank.

## What we store

We store the details needed to manage your stay: name, email, phone number, booking history and stated preferences (for example a preference for a high floor). Conversations are kept for 90 days for quality and safety review.

## Your data rights

You can ask for a copy of your data or ask us to delete it by emailing privacy@aurora-bay.example. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days.

## Booking security

To look up, change or cancel a booking we always need both the booking reference and the email address used to book. We never confirm whether a booking exists to someone who cannot provide both.
