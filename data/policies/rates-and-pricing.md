# How Our Rates Work

Aurora Bay Hotel (fictional demo property). Version 2026-11.

## Base rates

Base nightly rates per room: Standard Queen Room 140 USD, Deluxe King Sea View 210 USD, Family Suite 320 USD, Penthouse Suite 750 USD.

## Weekend and seasonal adjustments

Friday and Saturday nights are 15% above the base rate. Nights in the festive peak season (20 December to 5 January) are 30% higher. Nights in the low season (1 February to 15 March) are 10% lower.

## Demand-based pricing

Rates follow demand. When a room type is 65% or more booked on a night, that night is 10% higher; at 85% or more it is 20% higher. Booking early usually gets a better price.

## Longer stays

Stays of 4 or more nights get 5% off the room subtotal; stays of 7 or more nights get 12% off.

## Promotion codes

WINTER10 gives 10% off stays that start between 1 December 2026 and 28 February 2027. STAYLONG gives 8% off stays of 5 or more nights. Promotion codes apply after any longer-stay discount.

## Extra guests and taxes

Guests beyond the room's standard occupancy pay 40 USD per night. A 10% tax applies to the discounted subtotal. Quotes are valid for 30 minutes.
