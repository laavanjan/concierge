# Check-in, Check-out and During Your Stay

Aurora Bay Hotel (fictional demo property). Version 2026-11.

## Check-in time

Check-in starts at 15:00. Early check-in from 12:00 is available on request for 35 USD, subject to room readiness. Guests must be 18 or older to check in and must show a government photo ID.

## Check-out time

Check-out is by 11:00. Late check-out until 14:00 costs 40 USD and must be requested at the front desk the day before departure.

## Incidentals deposit

On arrival we pre-authorise 100 USD per room on a card for incidentals. The hold is released within 7 days of check-out.

## Quiet hours and smoking

Quiet hours are 22:00 to 07:00. The whole property is smoke-free, including balconies; smoking in a room incurs a 250 USD deep-cleaning fee.

## Accessibility

Two Standard Queen rooms are fully accessible with roll-in showers and grab rails. All floors are served by a lift. Mention accessibility needs in your booking's special requests.
